--- title: "NiTi Point Defects and Thermodynamic Modeling" excerpt: "Master's thesis work on point defects in NiTi, combining molecular dynamics, equation-of-state fitting, and thermodynamic analysis across temperature and composition." collection: portfolio ---

This project corresponds to my master's thesis, titled Advanced Study of Nickel-Titanium Alloy: Effects of Point Defects on Mechanical and Thermodynamic Properties.

I investigated how substitutional, antisite, and vacancy defects influence the mechanical and thermal response of shape-memory alloy NiTi in the B19' martensitic and B2 austenitic phases. The work relied on molecular dynamics with a second nearest-neighbor modified embedded atom method potential across temperatures from 200 K to 600 K and compositions from 45 to 55 atomic percent nickel.

The workflow combined automated job generation, large parameter sweeps, equation-of-state fitting, and thermodynamic post-processing. In total, the study covered 45,045 simulations, allowing systematic comparison of defect concentration, lattice parameter, temperature, and composition.
